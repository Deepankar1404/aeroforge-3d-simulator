🚀 How to Design, Launch & Reach Orbit

1. Design in the 3D VAB: Pick a Nose Cone/Capsule, toggle an Upper Orbital Stage, and size your Main Booster and Engine Cluster. Watch two key numbers in the Flight Readiness Analysis card:

  • Liftoff TWR > 1.20: Ensures your rocket has enough thrust to overcome gravity at liftoff.
  • Total Δv ≥ 4,200 m/s: Enough total velocity change to climb above the 100 km atmosphere and accelerate horizontally into a stable orbit!

2. Launch & Staging (SPACEBAR): Click Roll Out to 3D Launch Pad. Press SPACE (or click the orange button) to ignite Stage 1. When a stage runs out of fuel, press SPACE again to jettison the empty stage and ignite Stage 2!

3. Easy Orbit with Auto Gravity Turn: Leave the SAS computer on Auto Turn—it automatically pitches the rocket eastward as you climb so both your Apoapsis (Ap) and Periapsis (Pe) rise above 100 km for a permanent orbit!
